looks stale.

- [Services and ports](services-and-ports.md): the Tailscale Services hosted here, who may reach them, and the LAN ports.
- [UPS and NUT](ups-and-nut.md): the UPS, the NUT chain, shutdown timers per host, files, vars, health checks.
- [Synology NAS](nas.md): DSM, the serve config, shared folders, NFS exports, compose projects.
- [Home Assistant](home-assistant.md): both instances, their URLs, backups and telemetry.
- [Monitoring](monitoring.md): SLOs, probes, scrapes, alerts and dashboards for this System.
